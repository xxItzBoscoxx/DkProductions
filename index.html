Our Story

Rooted in Inwood

DK Productions began as a small group of local artists coming together to share their creations. As that community grew, so did the vision: to bring local talent beyond those initial gatherings and into neighborhood spaces where more people could discover and appreciate their work. Today, we host community events at local establishments, creating opportunities for artists, chefs, small businesses, and creatives to showcase what they do best. At our core, we're about celebrating the people, creativity, and connections that make Inwood special.
