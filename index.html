Our Story

Rooted in Inwood

Rooted in Inwood, NY, we’re a community-run organization dedicated to amplifying the visibility of local artists, chefs, small businesses, and other creatives. What began as a small gathering of local artists sharing their work has grown into a community-driven initiative hosting events at neighborhood establishments, creating opportunities for local talent to connect, showcase their creations, and gain recognition.

Be Part of Our Community

We’re proud to support the local artists, small businesses, chefs, and creatives who make our community unique!

From handmade creations and creative services to local food and small businesses, we’re passionate about giving neighborhood talent a platform to share their work and connect with the community.

Some of the amazing local creators we support include: Outlaw Designs, Creative Kitty, The Eye, The Plant Lab NYC, FireHouse BBQ, and many more!
